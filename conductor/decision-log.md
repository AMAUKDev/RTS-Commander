### DECISION-003: Map-driven AI variety, points layer first
- **Date**: 2026-09-13
- **Track**: strategic-points_20260913
- **Decision**: AI variety comes from geography (resource sites, villages, hilltops, bases), not personalities or deeper adaptation. Resource sites = existing industrial buildings + generated fill; mines only at sites; control points pay only while garrisoned by ≥ MinGarrison ground vehicles; per-base flat income. Delivered as four tracks: points (this), platoons with objectives, truck logistics, air/naval support tasking.
- **Rationale**: User observation: enemy AI mine-spams at its base and convoy-rushes one road. KISS: one new concept ("point") that every later system reads. Take-and-hold at platoon scale is the desired feel.
- **Alternatives**: Spacing rule only (no objectives to fight over); points as destroyable buildings (bombing targets, not held ground); personalities.
- **Impact**: New `Points` settings section, new strategic-points service, two AI decisions changed, COMMANDER LOG window, point markers on map and world.

### DECISION-005: Platoon operations doctrine
- **Date**: 2026-09-13
- **Track**: platoon-operations_20260913
- **Decision**: Missions-and-requisitions architecture (approach A). The mod claims every AI-bought vehicle at the depot; platoons of 6 (3 armour, 1 carrier, 2 AD) fill FOB, picket and attack missions; offensives run on 2–3 equidistant axes, sized to the commander's own tracked picture, with release points and a pressure clock (attack at least every 12 min). Buyer fills an order book of requisitions. AI-only this track.
- **Rationale**: Today's behaviour is a trickle to points plus one convoy at the nearest enemy. Explicit missions are legible in the COMMANDER LOG and give offensives a shape. Contact-weighted point value, axis-only manning gates and the pressure clock stop the sides expanding outward without meeting.
- **Alternatives**: Extend the garrison step and redirect game convoys (no scaling, still one stream); per-vehicle utility scoring (never forms up).
- **Impact**: New Operations/ service replacing the garrison partial and the home guard's recruit logic; buyer gains requisition mode; new markers and log block; new OPERATIONS settings.

### DECISION-006: Truck logistics shape
- **Date**: 2026-09-13
- **Track**: truck-logistics_20260913
- **Decision**: Mines and every control point pay only by truck; base income stays per tick. One persistent truck per paying point, round-trips to the nearest held base, pays on entering the ring, returns empty. A lost truck loses only its load and is replaced free on the next cadence. 3-minute loads.
- **Rationale**: User wants traffic both ways and a raidable economy without escort rules or a cost spiral. FOBs from the platoon track already sit on front-line points.
- **Alternatives**: Mines only (too little traffic); everything including base income (too much); truck consumed on arrival (one-way traffic); truck fee per spawn (can go net negative under pressure); escorts by rule (couples to platoons).
- **Impact**: New Logistics/ service, hopper accrual replacing direct payout for mines and control points, truck markers, IN TRANSIT readouts, Logistics settings section.

### DECISION-007: Air support tasking driven by the ground plan
- **Date**: 2026-09-13
- **Track**: air-support-tasking_20260913
- **Decision**: The operations mission list is the single source of air objectives — no separate air brain. CAS-only sorties over contested objectives first (platoon in contact or live attack), then front ForwardBase missions by threat; pickets and rear points get none. Magnitude scales with `CountObserved` + `ObservedFloors` (ladder 0→0, ≤2→1, ≤5→2, ≤9→3, ≥10→4 airframes; cap 4 CAS + 1 CAP escort per objective; wing ceiling 8 everywhere). CAP escort only when hostile air is tracked inside the objective ring (`ThreatMemorySeconds` freshness). The player-side AI tasks only airframes it bought itself; player AIR missions are never retasked. Launches ride the existing `BuyAirframe`/`TryLaunchAiAircraft` path on the existing `AirFund`; the duel-only gates on the air buy and task legs are removed so it runs on every commanded HQ, stock missions included. Losses re-request only while contested, behind a 2-min cooldown doubled to 4 when ≥2 hostile air-defence units are observed at the loss. Naval out of scope.
- **Rationale**: Today the enemy wing orbits the opponent's frozen opening airbase and only exists on the duel map. CAS bound to the platoon plan makes the air force fight over the same ground the platoons fight over, and the existing launch, recovery and budget machinery applies unchanged.
- **Alternatives**: A smarter fixed strike target in `TaskAirWing` (still a separate brain); commander launches through `TrySpawnFromRecipe` for recipe AUTO-relaunch (local-HQ + raw-funds path that bypasses the AirFund); scaling per platoon rather than per objective (more sorties than a wing of 8 can fly).
- **Impact**: New `Operations/CommanderOperationsAir.cs` partial on the operations service; a pending-launch claim notify on the existing `RegisterFactionUnit` postfix; `ChooseAirRole` reads sortie demand; duel gates deleted; 2 new config entries (Operations section); SelfCheck ladder cases; no new Harmony patches.

### DECISION-008: Picket insertion by transport helicopter rides the SAM supply machinery
- **Date**: 2026-09-13 (user-approved same day with four answers folded in)
- **Track**: heli-picket-insertion_20260913
- **Decision**: Rear picket points farther than 2 km from the nearest road (the user's gate, verbatim intent "more than 2km from road is fine") are filled by air instead of by road; a roadless map flies every picket. The operations service requests a transport heli, the two picket vehicles are bought as cargo mounts at heli spawn (charged their `cargo.value` each) — one air-defence plus the cheapest other mountable ground vehicle, per the user's "doctrine over bargains" answer — and the heli is rented (charged at spawn, refunded on recovery via the existing `PurchasedWithFunds` chain), flown to the point's hold-post ring by the existing `AIHeloTransportState` patches, landed and unloaded only (airdrop resolved out of scope), RTB'd and recovered. One insertion airborne per HQ, one request per review, 10-minute loss cooldown per point (confirmed); a lost heli loses its hull cost and the two vehicles aboard (engine behaviour: `MountedCargo.OnPartDetached` spawns cargo disabled). Funded from the ground pot, never the `AirFund`, `BuyAirframe` or `TaskAirWing` — the air-support wing (DECISION-007) and insertions buy and fly separate aircraft. Road distance is measured against the road polylines discovery already caches (`roadPointLists`, retained past discovery instead of cleared).
- **Rationale**: Pickets drive from the depot over the road network, so roadless rear points (hilltops, remote villages) pay nothing for most of the match and yield to platoons on the pool guard. The SAM supply feature already proved the whole buy-fly-land-unload-return chain in play; the only engine unknowns the capture ponytail flagged are asset data (which vehicles are mountable cargo), resolved by a once-per-mission roster log with drive-fill as the fallback.
- **Alternatives**: Gate on distance from the nearest held asset (design's first proposal, 10 km — the user replaced it with the road-distance gate: distance-to-road is what actually predicts a slow drive); physically loading pool vehicles aboard (no game API — loading is loadout at spawn, verified in the assembly); spawning the vehicles at the point when the heli arrives (teleports combat power, no delivery risk); sling loading (player-flown mechanic, no AI hook); airdrop variant (parachute-capable mounts exist but descent is unverified in play — user kept it out of scope).
- **Impact**: New `Operations/CommanderOperationsInsertion.cs` partial; road polylines retained past discovery (`Points/CommanderStrategicPointDiscovery.cs` — the `StepBases` clear removed) plus a `NearestRoadDistanceMeters` helper on the points service; an HQ-parameterised AI entry generalising `CommanderSupplyHeliService`'s local-HQ spawn gate (second caller after `RequestSamSiteFoundationDrop`); insertion branches in `HoldDeployedCargo`, the ejection suppression and `PruneFinishedMissions`; `PicketMembers` adoption of deployed vehicles; `heli=` on the `Ops … review:` line (the air-support track extends the same line with `air=[…]` — reconcile at implementation); 4 new Operations config entries, 1 new slider row, SelfCheck cases.

### DECISION-009: One spending ladder — strict home CAP, then a weighted draw
- **Date**: 2026-09-14
- **Track**: commander-priorities_20260914
- **Decision**: Every commanded HQ spends from one pot per review in a fixed ladder: (1) home CAP, strict — 2 fighters with active-radar-homing missiles over its airbases, plus one per two tracked enemy aircraft within 30 km (no upper limit), plus one per CAP fighter lost to enemy air in the last 10 minutes; nothing below is bought while CAP is short. (2) Platoons and their air support, (3) air-delivered pickets, (4) buildings share the remainder by a weighted draw each review (60/20/20) with a 10 % floor for any rung with open demand. The economy service's structure loop and the separate air fund stop spending on their own; both become rungs.
- **Rationale**: Four unconnected pots made priority emergent; on a map this size the platoon order book is never empty, so pickets and buildings were rarely reached, and nothing guaranteed a fighter screen over the commander's own base. The user asked for CAP first and "some variability between each decision loop", not between commanders.
- **Alternatives**: Retune the existing pot shares (cannot make CAP strict or guarantee lower rungs); serve one rung per review in rotation (lumpy; platoons wait two reviews in three); per-commander personalities (explicitly not wanted).
- **Impact**: `Ai/CommanderEnemyCommanderService.cs` review becomes the single spend site; `GetEnemyBuildReserve` no longer withholds; `AccrueFund`/`AirFund` retired; new settings `HomeCapBaseline`, `HomeCapPerEnemyAircraft`, `LadderPlatoonWeight`, `LadderPicketWeight`, `LadderBuildingWeight`, `LadderRungFloorPercent`; one `ladder:` log line per review.

### DECISION-010: Smarter air wing — ordnance, helicopter CAS, packages, AWACS, ARAD, idle sweep
- **Date**: 2026-09-14
- **Track**: smarter-air-wing_20260914
- **Decision**: CAS loadouts strongly prefer AGM-68 and AGM-48; attack helicopters are the CAS airframe for forward bases, pickets and platoons in contact while jets fly attacks and pre-emptive cover; sorties with more than one airframe form up at an orbit 12 km from base and go in together (bounded by a 3-minute wait); one AWACS per commander orbits 30 km behind the front; three or more tracked enemy air-defence vehicles within a 5 km cluster near an objective open an anti-radiation sortie ahead of that objective's CAS; every commander-owned airframe without a mission is retasked each review.
- **Rationale**: Sorties arrived one aircraft at a time because each airframe was sent the moment it launched; CAS carried whatever the generic scorer picked; no radar or anti-radiation employment existed; owned airframes could idle.
- **Alternatives**: Parallel multi-base launches (rejected by the user in favour of a form-up orbit); helicopter choice by distance to a friendly base (user chose by mission kind); a separate wing-planner service (duplicates the sortie table).
- **Impact**: New sortie kinds (Awacs, Arad, package state) in `Operations/CommanderOperationsAir.cs`; ordnance preference in the CAS scorer; settings `PackageFormUpSeconds`, `RotaryCasRangeMeters`, `AradClusterMinimum`.

### DECISION-011: Airframe selection by fitness tier
- **Date**: 2026-09-14
- **Track**: airframe-selection_20260914
- **Decision**: Airframes are ranked per role into tiers from the game's own anti-air / anti-surface ratings (Fighter, Multirole, Strike, LastResort; reversed for CAS). The highest tier a held base can launch always wins; within it the commander buys the best-rated airframe when the threat is high and funds allow, the cheapest when quiet; a tier is never dropped for price, only when nothing above it can launch. Applies to every commander airframe pick, including the choice among already-owned airframes and the idle sweep.
- **Rationale**: The same-day capability change let any airframe that could hang an air-to-air missile fly CAP on price, so Crickets and Compasses flew CAP with Revokers available, and fighters could be sent to CAS ahead of Brawlers.
- **Alternatives**: Hand-written per-airframe lists (brittle); a pure fitness/price score (what produced the problem).
- **Impact**: Tier table and selection rule beside the role buyer in `Ai/CommanderEnemyCommanderAir.cs`; tier shown on `Air roster` and launch lines; constants `FighterRatio` 1.5, `CapThreatAircraft` 2, `CasThreatObserved` 3.

### DECISION-012: Ground tactics — wide ring, defence arc, bounding advance, counter-attack
- **Date**: 2026-09-14
- **Track**: ground-tactics_20260914
- **Decision**: A holding platoon spreads on the point's full hold radius (air defence inside), forms a defence arc toward the threat with tanks and IFVs forward when contact is detected, and returns to the ring 60 s after contact lapses. An attacking platoon leaves the road at its release point and advances in 800 m cross-country bounds in line, waiting for half the platoon to close each bound, and never paths a road within 1 km of a tracked enemy. A reinforcing platoon arriving at a point that is already held counter-attacks the tracked enemy from a flank 1 km off the threat bearing, or screens 800 m out on the most threatened approach when no enemy is tracked, folding into the ring only if the garrison drops below minimum.
- **Rationale**: Posts at 60 % of the radius bunched the platoon; reinforcements shared the same posts; attackers drove the road into a defended point in column because the contact drill fires only on a tracked hostile.
- **Alternatives**: Approach-covering ambush posts (user chose ring default + adaptive arc); reverse-slope posts (too much terrain sampling); a separate tactics service (duplicates the move seam).
- **Impact**: `EnsureHoldPosts` / `DriveToHoldPosts` in `Operations/CommanderOperationsFront.cs`, posture handling in the movement tick, bounding in `CommanderOperationsOffensive.cs`, new marker texts; settings `DefenceArcStandoffMeters` 400, `BoundMeters` 800, `OffRoadRangeMeters` 1000.

### DECISION-013: Pickets first — pickets capture, platoons only toward the enemy, book-only buying
- **Date**: 2026-09-14
- **Track**: pickets-first_20260914
- **Decision**: Two-vehicle pickets capture and hold every control point that is not a front point and take pool vehicles ahead of new platoon formation. A platoon forms only for a purpose facing the enemy — a forward base on a front point, an attack, or one standing reserve — and dissolves to the pool when its purpose resolves. Ground buying is order-book only once the operations service owns the force; the platoon cap is deleted. Up to three insertion flights airborne and three requests per review. Reinforcement platoons per request 6; home CAP max 4 and the 10-minute insertion cooldown unchanged.
- **Rationale**: Platoons were formed from whatever the pool held and the plan buyer kept filling the pool, so 27 platoons spread over a map with no enemy near most of them, while the 8-platoon cap never bound (any open requisition lifted it) and pickets starved behind platoon formation.
- **Alternatives**: Fixing the cap's "lifts while open" condition (keeps the spam, just smaller); a per-commander vehicle budget (indirect).
- **Impact**: `PlanPickets`/`FillPickets`/`PlatoonsNeedThePool`/`TryFormPlatoon` in `Operations/CommanderOperationsFront.cs`, `GroundBuyingCapped` → `GroundBuyingBookOnly` in `Operations/CommanderOperationsRequisitions.cs` and the buyer, `PlanInsertions` limits, `OperationsReservePlatoons` default 1, `OperationsHeliInsertionLimit` default 3, `MaxReinforcementPlatoons` 6.

### DECISION-015: FOB cap, spacing, abandonment, and the captured-depot handover
- **Date**: 2026-09-14
- **Track**: fob-construction_20260914
- **Decision**: A commander owns at most `FobMaxPerCommander` (2) forward operating bases, counted off
  the live base list so a captured one counts against its captor; sites must be `FobMinSpacingMeters`
  (20 km) from any other FOB as well as 15 km from any airbase; only the front-nearest held point is
  ever a candidate; the order opens only when the building rung has banked the full price; losing one
  stops all FOB building for `FobLossCooldownMinutes` (15). At the cap, a quiet and unused rear FOB at
  least `FobAdvanceMeters` (20 km) further from the enemy than the candidate point is demolished and
  its base torn down, at most once per 10 minutes, so the next order can go in at the front. On
  capture, a depot is re-registered with its new owner, and no faction's deployment loop will spend
  supply at a depot it does not own.
- **Rationale**: The user's "we don't want EVERY capturable point turning into a FOB" and "we should
  also be able to abandon a FOB and then build one closer to the front-line". The depot handover
  closes a game bug where the losing faction bought vehicles that spawned for the winner.
- **Alternatives**: Any front point as a candidate (rejected: a whole line of candidates); a Harmony
  hook on `Airbase.CaptureFaction` for the handover (rejected: the event fires before the buildings
  change hands and before a depot knocked out in the fight is repaired, so a one-shot re-registration
  is pruned and never retried).
- **Impact**: `Operations/CommanderOperationsFob.cs`, `Economy/CommanderFobBuilder.cs`,
  `Depot/CommanderFactionVehicleService.cs`, three settings, one new self-check entry point.

### DECISION-014: FOB construction by air or road; depots on captured airbases
- **Date**: 2026-09-14
- **Track**: fob-construction_20260914
- **Decision**: A commander may order one FOB at a time on a control point it holds that is at least 15 km from any airbase and nearest the front: three deliveries (transport helicopters carrying construction cargo, or three munitions trucks by road) converge, then the mod creates a real runtime airbase (400 m capture ring, capturable) and spawns a vehicle depot, a radar and a helipad inside its build ring. Trucks are consumed; helicopters return and are recovered. Fixed recipe. Any captured airbase lacking a depot gets one first from the building rung, then a helipad if it lacks a pad.
- **Rationale**: The game exposes a public runtime airbase creator and a depot registers itself with the faction, so a FOB becomes a genuine spawning base with no Harmony patch; captured bases were unused for spawning when they had no depot.
- **Alternatives**: Destroy-only FOBs (rejected: capturable is worth fighting over); vehicles kept as garrison (rejected for trucks); commander-chosen recipes (rejected for now); helicopter building cargo (does not exist).
- **Impact**: New `Operations/CommanderOperationsFob.cs` partial, supply-heli construction cargo branch, ladder building-rung wish order, settings `FobMinBaseDistanceMeters`, `FobEnabled`.

### DECISION-016: Nearest-first tasking; a forward base's truck follows its platoon
- **Date**: 2026-09-14
- **Track**: platoon-operations_20260913 (addendum), pickets-first_20260914
- **Decision**: (1) A forward base gets a munitions truck dispatched, and posts a truck buy, only once at least one of its platoons is Holding the point; a base whose platoons have all left returns its truck to the pool. The truck chosen is the pool truck nearest the point. (2) A road picket fills from the pool vehicle nearest its point, armour last, and never a munitions truck. (3) A forward-base or attack mission takes the nearest available platoon, not the first in list order.
- **Rationale**: User report: "individual trucks driving around, often straight into the enemy by themselves" and tasking that "seems random, rather than towards closest". Two sources of lone trucks were found: every planned forward base drew and bought a truck from the review it was planned (15 bought, most to `0/2` bases), and the picket fill took the pool's cheapest vehicle by value, which is the truck (value 2).
- **Alternatives**: Escorting the truck inside the platoon's formation (deferred: the platoon movement seam does not carry a non-member); keeping cheapest-first for pickets with trucks excluded (rejected: still ignored distance).
- **Impact**: `Operations/CommanderOperationsFront.cs` (`FillMissionTrucks`, `AssignPlatoons`, `FillOnePicket`, `ForwardBaseWantsTruck`, `PicketCandidateRank`), `Operations/CommanderOperationsRequisitions.cs` (truck line gate); five new self-checks. New log lines: `<base>: truck follows <platoon> to the forward base (n km)`, `<base>: no platoon holds the point; its truck returns to the pool`.


### DECISION-017: Air buying is money-limited; suppression flies first and flies armed
- **Date**: 2026-09-14
- **Track**: smarter-air-wing_20260914 (departure)
- **Decision**: (1) The wing's buy loop continues while its funds cover the cheapest airframe an
  OPEN demand wants and the airborne count is under `AirborneCeiling`; `MaxAirBuysPerReviewSafety`
  (12) is a runaway guard only. This **supersedes the decision of 2026-09-13 that set
  `MaxAirBuysPerReview` to 3**. (2) When the ground buyer is held to its order book, that book is
  empty and the wing has an open request, the ground's unspent share of rung 2 (less the naval ask)
  is accrued into the air fund, bounded by the fund's existing ceiling. (3) An objective whose ring
  shows `AirDefenceHesitationCount` (2) or more tracked air-defence vehicles gets jet CAS only until
  an anti-radiation sortie has been on station over the belt; the memory is dropped when the belt
  thins below the threshold. (4) An aircraft may be bound to a suppression sortie only when its LIVE
  weapon stations carry an anti-radiation store, and a commander-built suppression loadout takes a
  real anti-radiation missile (ARMSeeker: ARAD-116, ARAD-45) on every pylon that accepts one and
  fills the rest with AGM-99, then AGM-68, then any other ground store.
- **Rationale**: The 2026-09-14 match logged `air demand: CAP 0/13, CAS 1/33, ceiling 13/20,
  budget 214` with the balance climbing past 600 and only two to four launches a review, and
  `holds: balance 491, unit budget 152, 7 vehicle types on offer (5 quiet reviews)` beside it.
  Cheap SAH-46 Chicanes were fed into seven to twenty-three observed air-defence vehicles until
  every sortie read `cooldown`. And an `FS-12 Revoker` tasked with an anti-radiation strike had been
  bought through the FIGHTER role path, so it flew air-to-air missiles against the belt: the binding
  test asked what the TYPE could carry, not what the aeroplane was carrying.
- **Alternatives**: Raising the per-review cap from 3 to a larger number (rejected: the same
  arbitrary limit, differently placed); making the air fund unbounded rather than handing the
  ground's surplus over (rejected: the ceiling is what stops the wing hoarding from the ground);
  opening the anti-radiation sortie at the hesitation count rather than at `AradClusterMinimum`
  (rejected: two separate tuned thresholds, and the cluster rule is the one that decides whether a
  belt is worth a sortie at all); ranking AGM-99 and AGM-68 as the PRIMARY suppression ordnance as
  the user's wording suggested (rejected on the game's own data — `resources.assets` describes the
  AGM-99 as an anti-ship missile and the AGM-68 as an optically guided missile, and neither carries
  an `ARMSeeker`, so making either primary would have made every strike aircraft a suppression
  candidate and sent sorties against radars with nothing that homes on one; they are ranked first
  among the SECONDARY stores instead, which is where the user's intent and the asset data agree).
- **Impact**: `Ai/CommanderEnemyCommanderAir.cs` (`MaxAirBuysPerReviewSafety`, the money-limited
  `AirBuyContinues`, `ReadAirDemandPrices`, `MayBindToRole`, `FillsAirRoleNow`, the launch note and
  `CheckAradOrdnancePreference`), `Ai/CommanderEnemyCommanderLadder.cs` (`GroundShareGoesToWing`),
  `Ai/CommanderEnemyCommanderService.cs` (the hand-over in `SpendPlatoons`, `GroundShareToWing`),
  `Operations/CommanderOperationsAir.cs` (`RotaryCasAllowed`, `AradMemorySurvives`, `LastAirDefence`,
  `AradFlown`, the five binding sites, the review line),
  `AirCommand/CommanderAirCommandScoring.cs` and `AirCommand/CommanderAirCommandLoadout.cs`
  (`PreferredAradSecondaryOrdnance`, `ScoreAradMountForCommander`, `CarriesAntiRadiation`,
  `AutoConfigureAradLoadout`). Twenty-nine new self-check cases.

### DECISION-018: The wing banks for what is asked; the airborne ceiling scales with income
- **Date**: 2026-09-14
- **Track**: smarter-air-wing_20260914, commander-priorities_20260914
- **Decision**: (1) The air fund's ceiling is the larger of the one-airframe rule and the cheapest wanted airframe times the number the open air demands are short, that number capped by the room left under the airborne ceiling. (2) The airborne ceiling is the configured value (20) as a floor plus one aircraft per `AirborneIncomePerAirframe` (15) of income a minute, up to `AirborneCeilingMax` (60), read from the same points-plus-mines income the diagnostics line prints.
- **Rationale**: With the count cap removed (DECISION-017) both commanders still bought one to two aircraft a review with thousands banked: the fund's ceiling was the price of ONE dearest airframe (`air saved 61 (cap 126)` beside `budget 8253`), and the player's side sat at `ceiling 20/20` with 34 CAP requests open. The user's standing instruction is "economy limited, not a hard cap".
- **Alternatives**: Raising the flat ceiling (rejected: a poor commander would then overfly its means); a balance-based ceiling (rejected: the balance is what the ladder is spending, so it would oscillate).
- **Impact**: `Ai/CommanderEnemyCommanderAir.cs` (five-argument `AirFundCeiling`, live overload reads `ReadAirDemandCounts`), `Operations/CommanderOperationsAir.cs` (`AirborneCeilingFor`, `EffectiveAirborneCeiling`), every ceiling reader in the buyer and ladder, two settings, ten self-check cases.

### DECISION-019: Idle-pool cap on automatic deployment; downed pilots auto-recovered
- **Date**: 2026-09-14
- **Track**: pickets-first_20260914 (pool), new core service (pilots)
- **Decision**: (1) The game's depot deployment loop is held for a commander the operations service manages once its idle pool reaches `PoolIdleCap` (12); factory supply banks at the depot. (2) A new core-tier service sweeps every 15 s and hands any AI pilot on the ground for `DownedPilotRescueMinutes` (5) to `PilotDismounted.Capture` by the nearest friendly ground unit, the game's own rescue path. Human pilots are excluded.
- **Rationale**: User report: "frame-rate has slowly decayed ... a lot of downed pilots waiting for rescue that should slowly auto-clear up". Decompiled `Factory.ProduceUnit` adds supply, not vehicles; the depot loop then spawns whatever supply exists, and the pool reached 238 idle. `PilotDismounted` has no timeout of its own: a landed pilot persists until captured or killed.
- **Alternatives**: Despawning idle vehicles (rejected: destroys paid-for assets); killing landed pilots (rejected: reads as a loss and the game's own despawn is a plain destroy); stopping the AI from building factories (deferred: factories are a legitimate economy choice, the leak was the deployment).
- **Impact**: `Depot/CommanderFactionVehicleService.cs` (`DeploymentHeldForFullPool`), `Operations/CommanderOperationsRequisitions.cs` (`PoolCount`), new `Core/CommanderDownedPilotService.cs`, two settings, ten self-check cases.

### DECISION-020: Fewer, reachable control points; depot reach; FOBs extend reach; spawn at the nearest depot
- **Date**: 2026-09-14
- **Track**: reach-and-points_20260914
- **Decision**: (1) Discovery drops wooded points and points more than 2 km from a road, resource sites included, bases exempt; the cap falls to 48 points at 3 km spacing and per-point income triples. (2) A point farther than 20 km from every owned vehicle depot is out of reach: no ground staffing, helicopter insertion only. ### DECISION-022: Strategic points are carried across a hot reload, not rediscovered
- **Date**: 2026-09-14
- **Track**: strategic-points_20260913 follow-up
- **Decision**: The strategic-points service implements `ICommanderPersistState`. A snapshot carries every discovered point (kind, position, radius, label, wooded mark, a base's airbase display name, a site's mine by `PersistentID`) with its hold owner stored as a FACTION NAME rather than an `hqOrder` index, plus the retained road polylines flattened to x/y/z triples. A restore rebuilds the list, relinks the airbase by display name and the mine by id, resolves the owner name back to an index against a freshly refreshed `hqOrder`, and sets discovery to Done. - **Rationale**: User, 2026-09-14: "every rebuild moves objectives around and we lose existing platoons". - **Impact**: new `Points/CommanderStrategicPointPersist.cs` (`Snapshot`, `Restore`, `ToRecord`/`FromRecord` for a point and a road polyline, `OwnerIndexByName`, `FactionNameAt`, `IsWaitingForRestore`, `CommanderStrategicPointRecord`, `CommanderRoadPolylineRecord`), `Points/CommanderStrategicPointService.cs` (restore grace in `TickPersistent`, `ResetPersistState` in `ResetSession`, a string `Expect` overload), `Core/CommanderStateModels.cs` (two snapshot properties), `Core/CommanderStateStore.cs` (`IsHotReloadLoad` made internal so the check is not defined a third time). ### DECISION-029: The buyer counts the vehicles it already has, once, where they physically are
- **Date**: 2026-09-15
- **Track**: commander-priorities_20260914 / pickets-first_20260914 follow-up (vehicle buy/sell churn)
- **Decision**: Before the ground buyer orders, each role's open order-book total is reduced by what already covers it — vehicles bought earlier this review, factory supply banked at the depots for the buyer's catalogue definitions, and vehicles idle in the pool — paid off most urgent bucket first (`OpenRolesByPriority`, `PayOffCoverage`). The buyer's view sums each line's full `Wanted` and no longer subtracts the line's `Filled` tally, and the standing-reserve line no longer subtracts the pool total itself; a claimed vehicle is counted exactly once, as a pooled one. The idle-pool sale keeps back as many vehicles of a role as the book has open lines for (`PoolUnitWanted`, counted not blanket), and `PoolIdleSince` is pruned with the staging posts whenever a vehicle leaves the pool.
- **Rationale**: The 2026-09-15 match log showed three quarters of bought vehicles sold unused a minute later. Only `boughtThisReview` was subtracted and it is cleared each review, so an open line was re-bought every 30 s up to five a review; buys that banked supply never registered and never credited the line; the depot-side pool-full hold banked supply that flooded out when platoons formed; and a vehicle returning from a dissolved platoon carried its hour-old idle stamp into the same review's sale. - **Alternatives**: Keeping `Filled` in the buyer's view (rejected: the stall above); a per-role "in flight" counter decremented on claim (rejected: registration is synchronous with the spawn, so nothing falls between bought and pooled/banked, and a spawn that never registers would pin the counter); a blanket skip of every pool vehicle whose role has an open line (rejected: pins a pool over the cap, which now also holds the buyer, with no exit); removing the idle stamp at each of the five pool-removal sites (rejected: `PruneStagingPosts` already runs twice per review with a reused scratch list and is behaviour-equivalent).
- **Impact**: `Operations/CommanderOperationsRequisitions.cs` (`OpenRolesByPriority` signature, `Coverage`, `OpenAfterCoverage`, `PayOffCoverage`, `SumCoverage`, `SumOrderBookByUrgency`, `PostWithdrawingRequisitions`, `PruneStagingPosts`/`PruneNotInPool`, `CheckCoverage`), `Operations/CommanderOperationsService.cs` (`OperationsState.CoveredByRole`, `PoolUnitWanted`, `SellSurplusPool`), `Operations/CommanderOperationsFront.cs` (self-checks), `Operations/CommanderOperationsDiagnostics.cs` (`covered:` fields), `Ai/CommanderEnemyCommanderService.cs` (pool-full hold, `CommanderState.GroundPoolFull`, catalogue passed to the book).

### DECISION-030: Air attrition brake — escalate the tier, then halve the ceiling and hold the dying side
- **Date**: 2026-09-15
- **Track**: smarter-air-wing_20260914 (follow-up)
- **Decision**: Each commander keeps a ten-minute record of airframe launches and losses per side of the wing (fighter; strike, which includes helicopter CAS and suppression). ### DECISION-039c: One shared idea of a belt — the hold asks the question the sweep asks
- **Date**: 2026-09-16
- **Track**: air-survival-layer_20260916 (amends Layer 3 of DECISION-039)
- **Decision**: "A belt worth suppressing" is one pure rule, `BeltWorthSuppressing(clusteredLaunchers, clusterMinimum)`, living in `Operations/CommanderOperationsAirArad.cs` and read by both callers: `AradWanted`, which sizes the anti-radiation sortie, and `BeltHoldsSortie`, which decides whether a formation holds short of its objective. ### DECISION-042: A marching platoon or a driving picket is re-raised from a depot that has overtaken it, at no cost
- **Date**: 2026-09-16
- **Track**: none (direct user instruction, applied outside a track)
- **Decision**: ONE rule and one action with two callers. A platoon in `Moving` on a `ForwardBase` mission, and a `Picket` mission's ground-driving detachment (`PicketMembers`), are both removed from the map and raised again from the nearest owned depot when the same pure rule says the move is worth it: `CommanderOperationsService.ShouldReseatGroup(remainingDriveMinutes, depotDriveMinutes, savingMinutes, farMinutes, inContact, withdrawing, onCooldown)`. The saving must EXCEED `CommanderSettings.PlatoonReseatSavingMinutes` (new, 15) — exactly on the margin the group is left alone, the patient side of the boundary `PlatoonFlies` and `GroundLineAllowed` already keep — and the group must still be further out than `CommanderSettings.AirMobileDriveMinutes`, which is the mod's ONE idea of a drive that is too long and is also what keeps a group at or near its objective out of the rule. A group in contact (the platoon's `InContactUntil`, the picket mission's `ContactUntil`), one withdrawing, and one inside the thirty-minute `ReseatCooldownSeconds` churn guard (`OnReseatCooldown`) are never dissolved. A fifth term is the strict-progress guard (user decision 2026-09-16, after the rule fired 230 times across 24 groups in one match): the new depot must be STRICTLY nearer the objective than `CommanderReseatRecord.LastDepotMinutes`, the depot drive time the last re-raise moved this group to. Every re-raise therefore shortens the remaining journey, which makes a two-depot loop impossible by construction rather than merely slow. `CommanderReseatRecord.ResetForNewObjective` drops that memory wherever a group is given a new objective, which is `AttachPlatoon` for a platoon and never for a picket, whose objective is its own control point. The cooldown is kept alongside it: one limits how OFTEN a group is moved, the other limits WHERE it may be moved to. Both drive times come from the existing `DriveMinutes` with the existing `RoadDetourFactor` and `GroundSpeedMetersPerSecond`; the depot comes from the existing `CommanderEconomyService.TryNearestOwnedDepot`; the group's position comes from the existing `FirstLiveMember`. The shared decision is `TryPlanReseat` and the shared action is `ReseatGroup`; the bookkeeping is one `CommanderReseatRecord` held by both `CommanderPlatoon` and `CommanderOperationsMission`.
  **Money**: nothing is charged and nothing is refunded, for either caller. Each vehicle is removed through `CommanderEconomyService.DespawnUnit` (the mod's one definition of removing a unit from the game) and the SAME vehicle is handed back to the new depot — `VehicleDepot.TrySpawnVehicle` where the pad is free, otherwise `hq.ModifyUnitSupply(definition, +1)` plus `CommanderFactionVehicleService.ReserveDeployment` naming that depot, which is precisely what the commander's own ground buy does when a pad is busy (`Ai/CommanderEnemyCommanderService.cs`). `ReseatVehiclesInTransit` — banked minus returned, for the length of the existing five-minute deployment reservation — is read in four places so the transfer is invisible to the rest of the service: the empty-platoon sweep in `AssignPlatoons` keeps a platoon alive while its vehicles are between despawn and redeploy; `PostForwardBaseRequisitions` and `PostPicketRequisitions` count them as present so the buyer does not purchase the same group a second time; and `FillOnePicket` counts them as present so a re-raised picket does not immediately claim another far vehicle out of the pool and drive the same journey again.
  **What differs between the two callers**: only the container and the follow-up. A platoon is a standing object with a state, a leader and formation slots, so `AfterPlatoonReseat` puts it into `Forming` — the state this service already means by "gathering, not retreating", which keeps the losses rule off it while it is empty — beside the new depot on a `ChooseFormUpPoint`, and `ReleaseReseatedPlatoon` marches it out again on the existing `IsReadyToMoveOut`/`FormUpTimeoutSeconds` pair. A picket has no state machine at all: emptying `PicketMembers` IS the re-raise, and the existing `FillPickets` claims vehicles back out of the pool nearest-first as they redeploy. The log line names which: `re-raises 9TH PLATOON from FOB HILLTOP 5: …` and `re-raises the picket for RESOURCE SITE 25 from FOB HILLTOP 5: …`.
- **Rationale**: User, 2026-09-16: "we have situations where platoons are bought and sent to the enemy, but often a FOB is built closer to the enemy long before they're anywhere near. when that happens, the platoon should despawn and then be re-spawned from that FOB", and the same day: "platoon re-raising should also apply to pickets travelling by ground." The evidence is in the running log — `raises 9TH PLATOON air-mobile for HILLTOP 13: 40 km from the nearest depot, 58 min by road` — where the air-mobile gate already answers the same question for a group that has not been raised yet. This is that gate's ground twin for one already under way, deliberately sharing its threshold rather than introducing a second idea of "far". Selling the vehicles through the idle-pool path (`PoolSellRefundFraction`, 0.5) and re-buying would have cost half a platoon for moving one; buying replacements outright would have cost a whole one. Neither is honest when the commander already owns the vehicles.
- **Alternatives**: Two implementations, one per caller (rejected outright — Reuse rule 4; the margin, the far test, the contact guard, the churn guard and the money accounting are identical and only the container differs); refunding at the idle-pool sale fraction and letting the order book re-buy (rejected: a 50% loss every time a forward base opens, which would make the feature cost more than the drive it saves); leaving a re-raised platoon in `Moving` with zero members while the vehicles redeploy (rejected: the losses rule reads a one-vehicle platoon as below half strength and would send it home the review the first vehicle arrived); giving a picket the platoon's Forming/Moving treatment (rejected as paraphrasing one caller into the other — a picket genuinely has no state to come out of, and `FillPickets` already does the re-claim); including `Attack` missions (rejected: an attack's platoons are bound to an axis, a release point and a form-up timeout, and dissolving one mid-approach is a behaviour change nobody asked for); a per-base "re-raised once for this base" flag instead of a cooldown (rejected: it needs a second record keyed on depots that come and go, where a clock longer than the deployment reservation gives the same guarantee).
- **Impact**: new `Operations/CommanderOperationsReseat.cs` (`ReseatCooldownSeconds`, `ReseatInTransitSeconds`, `ShouldReseatGroup`, `OnReseatCooldown`, `ReseatNoPreviousDepot`, `ReseatRecordAfterNewObjective`, `ReseatVehiclesInTransit` and its record overload, `ReviewGroundReseats`, `TryPlanReseat`, `ReseatGroup`, `AfterPlatoonReseat`, `ReleaseReseatedPlatoon`, `CheckReseat`); `Operations/CommanderPlatoon.cs` (new `CommanderReseatRecord`, held by both `CommanderPlatoon` and `CommanderOperationsMission`); `Operations/CommanderOperationsService.cs` (the review call, the self-check call); `Operations/CommanderOperationsFront.cs` (the empty-platoon sweep guard, `FillOnePicket`'s wanted count, the strict-progress reset in `AttachPlatoon`); `Operations/CommanderOperationsRequisitions.cs` (the forward base's free-slot count, the picket's shortfall); `Core/CommanderSettings.cs` (`PlatoonReseatSavingMinutes`, 15, plus its warm-up touch); `CHANGELOG.md`.

### DECISION-045: A sortie's strength is what is assigned to it, it never holds over its own runway, every kind goes in whole, and every size is read through one envelope
- **Date**: 2026-09-16
- **Track**: sortie-commitment_20260916 (answers `conductor/designs/2026-09-16-sortie-standdown-investigation.md`; builds on `air-fallback-posture_20260916` and `air-survival-layer_20260916`)

The line is `<sortie>: no clear ground to hold — its fighters join the home patrol.` **(3)** `SortieIsPackage` is widened in place into `SortieFormsUp(kind, casWanted, capWanted, groundContact, aradGoneIn)` and the whole existing form-up machinery — `TryFindFormUpPoint`, `CountAtFormUp`, `PackageGoesIn`, `CommanderSettings.PackageFormUpSeconds` — now serves fighter patrols, ground-attack sorties and escorts as well as strike packages. The decision is taken ONCE, in `AddDemand`, and stored in `GoneIn`; `SortieHoldsAtFormUp` reads the stored answer rather than re-deriving it from counts the sizing rebuilds every thirty seconds. The reconcile's `wanted.GoneIn = live.GoneIn || wanted.GoneIn` is the way out of a hold. **(4)** `SortieElementWanted(floor, grown, cap)` is the one sizing envelope, generalised from `StrikeEscortWanted` and read by the escort rule, `CapWanted`, `CasWanted` and the falling-back sortie's `CallForFighters`. The first three retrofits are behaviour-neutral and a self-check loop pins that at every input from 0 to 12; the fourth gains a cap, the new class constant `FallbackFightersCap` = `2 * CapPerObjectiveCap` = 6.
- **The in-contact judgement, and why**: a sortie whose objective is in GROUND contact never forms up, and a sortie already gathering goes in the moment its objective comes into ground contact. Hostile AIRCRAFT tracked near the objective are deliberately NOT an exemption. `SortieIsInContact` reads true whenever anything hostile is tracked in the ring, so exempting on it would switch Part 3 off in precisely the fights it exists for — and the 96 sorties that ended `its fighters are gone` are what trickling fighters into hostile air costs. Ground contact is different in kind: a platoon under fire cannot wait three minutes, and `RetaskSourceRank` already ranks ground contact above cover. Both directions carry a self-check. The accepted cost is that a patrol over a quiet objective may now spend up to `PackageFormUpSeconds` (180 s) assembling where it used to be on station in about a minute.
- **Why the fallback call is capped rather than clamped by headroom**: `StrikeEscortWanted` clamps the escort by room under the airborne ceiling, and the same clamp on a patrol would have been wrong. Sortie demand also drives the RETASK, which moves aircraft that are already airborne and already under the ceiling, so a headroom clamp would stop a contested objective drawing fighters off quiet ones at exactly the moment the sky is full. The airborne ceiling is already enforced where aircraft are actually bought. A per-sortie cap answers the real absurdity, which was a median call for eleven fighters and one for thirty-one.
- **What was deliberately NOT changed**: what a QUIET objective asks for. The design's prose says "a quiet objective draws a pair"; the user's instruction says not to silently change it. It stays at one fighter and no ground-attack airframe, kept as the envelope's floor, with two self-checks pinning it. ### DECISION-046: An air-mobile platoon lift goes as one wave, and a cargo run now has an identity of its own
- **Date**: 2026-09-16
- **Track**: lift-wave_20260916
- **Decision**: (A) A platoon lift launches EVERY load it still owes in one review or launches nothing. The size of the wave is `LiftLoadsOutstanding(delivered, inFlight, loadsWanted)`, so the rule needs no special case for a top-up: a lift that loses one of three raises a wave of one next review under exactly the same gates. (B) Four things must all hold before any of it leaves the deck — room under the shared airborne ceiling for the WHOLE wave (`LiftWaveHasRoom`), funds for the WHOLE wave at the existing `LiftFundsMultiple` (`LiftWavePrice` handed to the existing `LiftAffordable`), the escort up by the existing `LiftCoverIsUp` and its existing bounded wait, and the existing hopeless/route gate. No new clock and no new setting: the user's instruction was explicit that the giving-up path is the one that already exists, and `CancelFobOrder` already hands a failed raise back to an ordinary driving platoon. (C) The one-cargo-run-per-point invariant is lifted by giving each run an identity rather than by keying on something else: `CommanderCargoFlightSlot.Unslotted` (zero) and `CommanderCargoFlightSlot.Matches(runSlot, wantedSlot)` are the one definition, and the id is carried on `QueuedCargoSpawn`, `PendingAircraftSpawn` and `CargoMission`. `CancelInsertion`, `TryRedirectInsertion`, `TryConvertInsertionToAirdrop`, `NotifyInsertionAircraft`, `NotifyPicketVehicleDelivered` and `NoteInsertionLaunchFailed` all take it, defaulting to zero — so every caller written before this (the player's supply window, the SAM foundation drop, the naval run, every picket insertion) keeps its point-wide behaviour unchanged, which a self-check pins. (D) A landing-zone move re-points the wave ONE LOAD AT A TIME, each to its own hold post through the dispatch's own `LiftPostIndex`, instead of one point-wide call with one target — three transports told to touch down on the same square metre is the defect the per-vehicle hold posts exist to prevent. (E) Forward-base construction is unchanged: `FobDeliveries` is one, so every new gate reduces to the expression it replaced, pinned by twelve self-checks including a ceiling sweep at every reading from 0 to 8.
- **Rationale**: User instruction 2026-09-16, explicit and approved: "should only happen if all 3 aircraft can fly at once to deploy full platoon in one flight (plus escort)." The marker read `1ST PLATOON — 0/3 delivered` for most of a quarter of an hour because `DispatchFobAirFlights` deliberately sent one load at a time, and its own comment said why. Verified against the current code before designing: five per-point calls genuinely break with more than one run on a point, and the worst of them is the delivery credit — `TryTakeFobDelivery` matched on the point alone, so every landed vehicle was credited to the FIRST of three records and the other two transports flying home would each have been counted as a lost load, which the first-loss rule would then have turned into a cancelled order whose platoon was already on the ground.
- **The pending-spawn slot, and why it did NOT have to be widened**: `pendingAircraftSpawn` holds exactly one spawn, and it looked like the hardest part. It is not, because it is transient: `TrySpawnCargoRunAtAirbase` sets it, registration clears it in the same frame, and requests arriving while it is occupied go on `queuedCargoSpawns`, which `TryProcessQueuedCargoSpawns` drains one per second on the supply tick. Three loads requested in one review therefore leave the ground within about two seconds of each other and cross the map together, which is a wave by any honest reading. Widening the slot to hold N would have been a real concurrency risk in code that has already caused two incidents (the airborne hand-off crash that stopped all transports spawning, and the "no transports spawning at all" regression), for no gain. Stated plainly rather than hidden: the wave is one REVIEW, not one frame.
- **What happens when only some transports are available**: nothing launches. The airborne ceiling gate asks for room for every load at once and says `the whole lift goes at once or not at all: it wants 3 transports and only 2 of 6 may fly`; the money gate asks for the multiple of the whole wave's price. If a load still fails to launch after all four gates passed — the roster or a base changed inside one review — the loads already away are recalled by their own slot ids through `WithdrawLiftFlight` and counted as launch failures rather than losses, and the order tries the whole wave again next review. Preferring to fail to launch over launching something that cannot be cleanly cancelled is the standing instruction for this code.
- **What happens when a wave is partly shot down**: the existing rules, unchanged, and they now bite on three aeroplanes rather than one. A transport lost in flight is removed by the existing sweep, counts one loss and leaves the other two records alone. The existing `FobOrderCanContinue` then cancels the ORDER outright if nothing has landed yet, which recalls the survivors and gives the objective back to a driving platoon — the first-loss rule is deliberately untouched, since changing it is a gameplay change nobody asked for, but it is a sharper rule against a wave than against a single flight and is called out in the changelog. A loss after something has landed replaces itself, and the replacement goes as its own smaller wave.
- **Alternatives**: Launching the wave and letting partial failures ride (rejected: it is the trickle the user refused, one review later); keying the supply side's per-point calls on the transport aircraft instead of a slot id (rejected: a queued request has no aircraft yet, which is exactly the window a recall has to reach); widening `pendingAircraftSpawn` to a list (rejected, see above); a new "wave" setting for how many loads may fly together (rejected, Reuse rule 4: `LiftLoadsPerPlatoon` already says how many loads a platoon takes and `HeliInsertionFlightsMax` already says how many may be airborne); a second give-up clock for a wave that cannot assemble (rejected by the user's instruction and unnecessary — the hopeless rule and `FobOrderTimeoutSeconds` already end the wait); leaving `CommanderCargoFlightSlot` as two members on `CommanderSupplyHeliService` (rejected for one concrete reason: that class's type initializer cannot run outside a game, so the rule could not have been self-checked, and it is a contract between two services rather than the supply side's own business).
- **Impact**: new `Supply/CommanderCargoFlightSlot.cs`; `Operations/CommanderOperationsFob.cs` (`LiftLoadsOutstanding`, `LiftWaveHasRoom`, `LiftWavePrice`, `LiftPostIndex`, `LiftFlightDetail`, `LiftWaveLine`, `TryLaunchLiftLoad`, `RecallPartialLiftWave`, `WithdrawLiftFlight` and the new `CheckLiftWave` block added; `DispatchFobAirFlights` and `RedirectLiftFlights` rewritten; `CommanderFobFlight.SlotId` and `.LoadOrdinal` added; five per-point calls narrowed to their flight); `Operations/CommanderOperationsService.cs` (`OperationsState.LastLiftSlotId`); `Operations/CommanderOperationsInsertion.cs` (the three notify entry points carry the slot); `Operations/CommanderOperationsAirMarkers.cs` (the per-transport detail reads the flight's own load number); `Supply/CommanderSupplyHeliMission.cs`, `Supply/CommanderSupplyHeliService.cs`, `Supply/CommanderSupplyHeliLandingZone.cs` (the slot carried and matched); `CHANGELOG.md`. ### DECISION-048: The bottom tier takes the easy jobs and pads an element the allocation cannot cover whole, and bleeding outranks both
- **Date**: 2026-09-16
- **Track**: cheap-aircraft_20260916 (builds on airframe-selection_20260914 / DECISION-011, strike-packages_20260915, faction-asymmetry_20260916 / DECISION-044 and sortie-commitment_20260916 / DECISION-045)
- **Decision**: Two rules, both approved in advance by the user ("i also want to see increased use of the cheap aircraft", then choosing BOTH of the two options offered and accepting more aircraft in the sky and more losses). **(1) EASY JOBS.** `AirJobIsEasy(enabled, admissible, trackedHostileAir, observedHostiles, observedAirDefence, objectiveInContact)` is one pure predicate reading only numbers the sortie is ALREADY sized from — `CommanderAirSortie.LastHostileAir`, `LastObserved`, `LastAirDefence` and `InContact` — and a buy for an easy sortie shops `AirframeTier.LastResort` ahead of whatever the ordinary order chose, the escort included. The line: nothing hostile tracked in the air over the objective, no air-defence vehicle observed near it, no more than `CheapAirframeMaxObserved` (2) hostile ground vehicles observed on it, and the objective not in contact. **(2) PADDING.** `ElementProperBuys` buys as many of the element's proper type as the allocation covers and `ElementPaddingSlots` hands the rest to `PadElementWithCheapAirframes`, which fills each remaining slot with `CheapestPaddingIndex`'s pick from the SAME candidate list the element itself was chosen from. **(3) PRECEDENCE.** `CheapAirframesAdmissible(forSortie, role, bleeding, suppressionSortieWaiting)` is the ONE shared veto behind both, so they can never disagree: no sortie asked, a suppression or radar role, a bleeding side, or a suppression sortie still waiting for its aeroplane, and neither rule fires. **(4)** Two config entries, `CheapAirframeEasyJobs` and `CheapAirframePadding`, both defaulting on; both off restores 2026-09-15 exactly.
- **Why bleeding wins, decided rather than left open**: the team lead's view was that bleeding should win as the newer and more specific signal; the reason recorded here is a different and stronger one. The attrition ledger is a measured fact about aircraft that have actually died in the last ten minutes, where "easy" is an ESTIMATE built from what the commander can currently see — and a side that is bleeding is precisely the side whose picture of the battlefield is wrong. It also closes the feedback loop this change opens, without a second rule to do it: more cheap aircraft mean more losses, more losses set the bleeding flag, and the flag switches both new rules off until the ledger recovers. The attrition read was moved ABOVE the tier choice in `TryBuyRole` for this, which is the only re-ordering in the buy.
- **Why the suppression budget is untouchable**: no cheap airframe on either faction's roster carries an anti-radiation missile (DECISION-044 leaves the ARAD-116 on the FS-12 Revoker at 65 and the KR-67 Ifrit at 126), a sortie behind a belt now WAITS for the sweep rather than flying it (DECISION-045), and the air fund is one shared per-review slice. A cheap airframe bought while the sweep is unbought is therefore a belt that stays up and a package that never goes in. The read is `ReadOpenAirDemandRoles`'s own `arad` flag, taken once per buy, not a second opinion about open demand.
- **What padding may never do**: displace an airframe the sortie could otherwise have afforded. The proper count is taken FIRST and is the most the allocation covers (`AffordableAirframes`, extracted from `PackageElementBuys` behaviour-neutrally); padding spends only the remainder. It also needs something to pad — with no proper airframe bought there is no element, and that case stays the 2026-09-13 rule that the commander saves rather than settling. Every existing limit still binds: the airborne ceiling is read before every padding airframe exactly as before every proper one, the diversity cap and the last-resort ranking are carried by `CheapestPaddingIndex`'s two passes, and the faction roster split and the role capability gate are already in the candidate list it reads. What padding DOES break, deliberately, is the one-type element rule of 2026-09-14: a mixed pair that flies beats a matched pair that was never bought. The element's pin is untouched, so the next review tops it up with the proper type.
- **Why distance from the enemy is not part of "easy"**: the only per-sortie distance the mod records, `CommanderAirSortie.EnemyDistanceMeters`, is set for a pre-emptive platoon sortie and left at -1 for every other kind, so a predicate every sortie passes through cannot honestly ask it. The smallest honest definition is the four facts every sortie already carries.
- **Alternatives**: Letting padding fire with NO proper airframe bought (rejected: it collapses the tier discipline into "the poor commander always flies trainers" and contradicts the 2026-09-13 save-for-it rule, where the easy-job rule answers the same case honestly and with a reason); a distance term in the easy predicate (rejected, see above); re-using `ThreatHigh` as the easy test (rejected: its thresholds are two tracked aircraft and three observed hostiles, so "not high threat" would call an objective with one enemy fighter over it easy); applying either rule to the home patrol's buy (rejected and recorded as out of scope: the home patrol is the side's last line of air defence, and its `HighCap` element rule already buys the most air-to-air per credit, which picks a cheap good fighter without needing to be told the job is easy); a second tier-override function beside `EscortTier` (rejected under Reuse rule 5 — `PreferTier` was extracted and `EscortTier` retrofitted onto it, and the existing escort self-checks pin that the retrofit is behaviour-neutral); forking the last-resort ranking so padding could buy a CI-22 Cricket over a T/A-30 Compass (rejected: the 2026-09-13 rule is one definition and `CheaperForPadding` keeps it).
